♿ Accessibility Route
Wheelchair-friendly route available via Gate 1 ramp access. Escort service provided by temple volunteers. Senior citizens get priority darshan.
♿ Full Accessibility Route Guide
1
Arrival: Drive to Gate 1 (main entrance). Dedicated accessible parking spots available within 50 meters.
2
Help Desk: Report at Gate 1 Help Desk. Request wheelchair (free) and volunteer escort. Show any disability certificate for priority pass.
3
Ramp Entry: Use the dedicated ramp on the left side of Gate 1. Flat, smooth surface — no stairs. Volunteer will guide you.
4
Priority Darshan: Direct access to Samadhi Mandir via accessible corridor. No queue wait — priority entry for wheelchair users & senior citizens (65+).
5
Rest Area: Shaded rest benches available near exit. Accessible toilet near Gate 1. Drinking water station with low-height tap.
6
Exit: Return wheelchair at Gate 1 desk. Escort will help you back to parking. Total route: ~0.8 km, flat surface, ~25 min.

What are the temple darshan timings?▼
Temple opens at 4:30 AM with Kaakad Aarti. Darshan continues until 10:30 PM. Closes briefly at 12:00–1:00 PM for Madhyan Aarti. Special timings apply on Ekadashi and festival days.
Is photography allowed inside the temple?▼
Photography is prohibited inside the Samadhi Mandir (inner sanctum). It is permitted in the outer courtyard and Ghat areas. Always be respectful of other pilgrims when clicking photos.
How do I reach Alandi from Pune?▼
Alandi is 24 km from Pune. PMPML buses run from Shivajinagar and Swargate (Bus No. 216 & 211). Shared autos from Dehu Road station. Private taxis available. Journey takes 45–60 min depending on traffic.
Is there a dress code?▼
Traditional and modest clothing is expected. Sarees, salwar-kameez or kurta-pyjama preferred. Shorts, sleeveless tops and revealing clothing should be avoided as a mark of respect.
What facilities are available for the elderly?▼
Wheelchair assistance available at Gate 1 and Gate 3. Priority darshan for senior citizens. Rest benches throughout the route. Medical volunteers on duty during peak hours.
Where can I leave my belongings safely?▼
Cloak rooms available near Gate 1 and Gate 3. Cost: ₹10–20 per item. Shoes must be deposited before entering temple — numbered token system ensures safe return.
Is mobile network available inside?▼
All major networks (Jio, Airtel, VI, BSNL) available. Congestion during peak crowd. Download offline maps before visiting. Free Wi-Fi zones near the main gate.
